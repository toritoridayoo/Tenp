---
name: Rank purchase modes
description: Product rules for rank purchase requests and payment intake.
---

Rank purchase intake is configurable per server between Booth and Amazon gift cards. Booth remains the default and keeps the existing flow. Amazon intake is only for rank requests; it asks for Minecraft ID, gift-card number, amount, and a written product description, then staff reviews the ticket and chooses the rank duration.

**Why:** the user explicitly requested server-level Booth/Amazon switching while preserving the current Booth behavior.

**How to apply:** keep other purchase flows unchanged unless asked, and do not treat Amazon submissions as automatically approved or granted.
